    Inside the lock on your door

    Five pins. One key.

    Pins set 0/5Plug 0°
    1. Spring, pin, pin

      Each of the five chambers holds a spring and two pins. With no key in, the top pins block the plug from turning.

    2. The key lifts them

      Every cut on your key pushes its pin to one exact height: the shear line, where the plug meets the housing.

    3. All five line up

      When every gap sits on that line, nothing is left blocking the plug, and it turns.

    4. That's a rekey

      Moved in? A rekey swaps those pins for new lengths. The old keys stop at the wrong height, your hardware stays put.
