How dark
can you go?

Start here. Tint is measured in VLT, the percent of visible light the glass lets through, so lower means darker. Florida sets the floor by window:

Tap a row to see it on the glass up top. Source: Fla. Stat. §316.2953 (front side windows) and §316.2954 (windows behind the driver; 6% applies to “multipurpose passenger vehicles”). Not sure which applies to your car? Ask Nick.
