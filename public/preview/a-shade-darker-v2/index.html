Questions

Straight answers.

How dark can I legally go in Florida?

Front side windows: 28% or lighter. Behind the driver: 15% or lighter on cars, and 6% or lighter on SUVs and vans.

Fla. Stat. §316.2953 and §316.2954. Not sure which applies to yours? Ask Nick.
What does the percentage mean?

It’s VLT, visible light transmission: the share of light that gets through the glass with the film on. A lower number is a darker window.

How long does a car take?

It depends on the car. One customer’s Camry: “I was in & out within an hour & a half!” Text Nick your year and model for yours.

I’ve never had tint. Can he help me pick?

That’s what customers say he does: “This was my first time getting a tint done, but he helped chose what would look best with my car.”

Do you do homes and businesses?

Yes. The shop describes itself as window tinting “for Automotive, Residential and Commercial.” Send a photo of the windows to start.

When are you open?

Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM, at 330 Mountain Dr in Destin. Closed weekends. One customer did write that he “even came to work on a Saturday to accommodate our schedule,” so it never hurts to ask.
